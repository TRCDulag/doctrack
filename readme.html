Document Tracking

Know where every paper document is, who received it, and when.

How a document moves

Each office picks the next office when it forwards. The paper goes with it.

Get your account

  1. Ask ICTU for an account.
  2. ICTU gives you a username and a temporary password.
  3. Your account is only for you. Do not share it.
2

Log in the first time

  1. Type your username and password, tap Log in.
  2. Choose your own password: 8 or more letters and numbers.
  3. On a phone, tap Install this app to put it on your home screen.
3

Send a document

  1. Tap New. Type the title, choose the type and the first office.
  2. Tap Log and get tracking number.
  3. Tap Print label, stick or print it on page 1, and bring the paper.
4

Receive a document

  1. On My office, tap Scan to receive and point at the QR label (or type the number, like 10-001).
  2. Tap Receive and sign.
  3. Sign with your finger, or type your PIN to use your stored signature.
5

Forward, return or complete

  1. Open the document and tap Release (forward / return / complete).
  2. Forward: pick the next office. Return: pick a reason. Complete: last stop.
  3. Bring the paper to the office you picked.
6

Find a document

  1. Tap Track, then type the number or tap Scan QR code.
  2. See every office it passed, who signed, and where it is now.
  3. You only see documents your office is part of.
7

Set up your signature and PIN

  1. Go to Account > Set up signature and PIN.
  2. Draw it, or Upload a picture of your signature (dark ink on white paper).
  3. Choose a PIN of 4 to 6 digits. 5 wrong PINs lock it; ICTU unlocks it.
