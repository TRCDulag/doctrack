Document Tracking: README

A short guide for staff. The app records where each paper document is, who received it and when, from the first office to the last.

1. Getting an account

Ask ICTU for an account. ICTU gives you a username and a temporary password in person or by private message. Each person has their own account; do not share it.

2. Logging in

  1. Open this app and type your username and password, then tap Log in.
  2. The first time, choose your own password: at least 8 characters, with letters and numbers.
  3. On a phone, tap Install this app on this device (or "Add to Home screen") so it opens like a normal app.

3. Sending a document

  1. Tap New. Type the title, choose the type, and pick the first office the paper goes to.
  2. Tap Log and get tracking number. The app gives the document a tracking number like DT-2026-10-001.
  3. Tap Print label and print the QR label on the first page of the paper, then bring the paper to that office.
  4. You can follow it any time under Sent.

4. Receiving a document

  1. On My office, tap Scan to receive and point the camera at the QR label. You can also type the number, for example 10-001.
  2. Tap Receive and sign. On a phone or tablet, sign with your finger. On a computer, use your stored signature and PIN.
  3. Documents with your office are listed under My office.

5. Forward, return or complete

When your office is done, open the document and tap Release (forward / return / complete). Choose what happened (for example Approved), then:

  • Forward to the next office you choose,
  • Return to the previous office or to the sender, with a reason, or
  • Complete if this is the last stop.

The paper goes with the record: bring it to the office you chose.

6. Finding a document

Tap Track and type the number, or tap Scan QR code. You see every office it passed, who signed, and where it is now. You can print the routing status report from there. You only see documents your office is part of.

7. Your signature and PIN

Go to Account > Set up signature and PIN. Either draw your signature, or upload a picture of it: sign in dark ink on plain white paper and take a clear photo or scan. Then choose a PIN of 4 to 6 digits. Your stored signature is used only when you type your PIN. After 5 wrong PINs it locks, and ICTU unlocks it.

8. Problems and help

  • Forgot your password, or your PIN is locked? Ask ICTU to reset it.
  • The app looks old after an update? On a computer press Ctrl+Shift+R. On a phone, close the app and open it again. The version is shown under the login box.
  • An error with a code like E-PIN-01? Tell ICTU the code.
  • Privacy: the app keeps only names, positions, offices, signatures and the times documents move (Data Privacy Act of 2012).
